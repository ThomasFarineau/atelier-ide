# Microsoft Office

Office documents open in a read-only viewer, with no office suite needed, the same on every OS, and export to PDF:

- Word (`.docx`): drawn as a page, with headings, character formatting, lists, links, tables and images;
- Excel (`.xlsx`): a grid with one tab per sheet;
- PowerPoint (`.pptx`): a column of slides.
